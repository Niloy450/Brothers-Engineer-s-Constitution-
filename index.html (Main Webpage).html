About Me & BEC Engineering

Bridging Structural Science with Modern Architectural Vision

I am Engr. Rofikunnobi, Founder of BEC — Brothers Engineer's & Construction. Based in West Vashantek, Dhaka Cantonment, our firm specializes in end-to-end civil engineering consultancy, soil investigation, structural analysis, and high-precision building construction.

In a rapidly urbanizing Bangladesh, safety and structural longevity are non-negotiable. Every blueprint we draft and every foundation we pour adheres strictly to the Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC) and seismic safety standards—ensuring peace of mind for homeowners, developers, and industrial clients alike.
